About DACDI

Developing People for a Better Future

DACDI focuses on practical learning, career development, leadership and professional skills for young people, professionals and communities.

Our Mission

To provide accessible and practical education, professional training and career development opportunities that empower individuals to build skills, confidence and meaningful careers.

Our Vision

To become a trusted center for professional education, career development and practical skills training, connecting learners with opportunities locally, across Africa and beyond.

Our Core Values

Integrity

Promoting honesty, responsibility and ethical conduct.

Excellence

Encouraging quality learning and continuous improvement.

Empowerment

Helping learners develop confidence and practical abilities.

Opportunity

Connecting skills development with career and professional opportunities.

Founder & Leadership

Dr Abdikadir Said Jama

Dr Abdikadir Said Jama is the founder of Dr Abdikadir Career Development Institute (DACDI), an initiative focused on education, professional development and practical career skills.

Through DACDI, the institute seeks to support learners with relevant knowledge, practical training and professional development opportunities.

Frequently Asked Questions

FAQ

Answers to some common questions about DACDI.

Are all DACDI programs currently open for registration?

No. Training opportunities are announced according to availability. Please check our announcements or contact DACDI for current opportunities.

How can I register for a training?

You can use the general application form on this website or contact DACDI through WhatsApp.

Where is DACDI located?

DACDI operates from Somaliland and provides learning opportunities through online and professional training activities.

How can I contact DACDI?

You can contact the institute by email, WhatsApp or through our official Facebook page.
